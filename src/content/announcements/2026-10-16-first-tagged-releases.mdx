## What is in them

**collector 0.2.0** is an [OpenTelemetry Collector distribution](/docs/operate/collector/) built with Collector components 0.160.0. Next to the upstream pieces (the `otlp` receiver, `basicauth` and `health_check`, the `batch`, `memory_limiter`, `attributes`, `resource` and `transform` processors, the `otlp`, `otlphttp` and `debug` exporters, the `routing` connector), it adds three of our own:

- the [`ouoreplay` receiver](/docs/operate/collector/#ouoreplay-receiver) on port 4319, which takes replay chunks at `POST /v1/replay/chunks`, and its exporter, which hands them to rum-api;
- the [`ouoratelimit` processor](/docs/operate/collector/#ouoratelimit);
- the [`ouosentry` receiver](/docs/operate/sentry-bridge/) on port 4320, which turns Sentry SDK envelopes into OTLP and can forward them to Sentry as well.

**rum-api 0.2.0** is the first image of the service that stores replays and serves sessions to Grafana.

**grafana 0.2.0** is Grafana 13.2.1 with our Grafana app inside, at version 0.6.0. That version added taps to Sentry video replays (an orange ring where the finger touched, with the component's name), tap markers on the scrubber, and an activity list beside the video and frame players.

## Supply chain

Every image is built for `linux/amd64` and `linux/arm64`, and carries an SBOM and a provenance attestation. Each release names its digest, listed above, so you can check that what you pulled is what was released.

## Which versions work together

collector 0.2.0 and grafana 0.2.0 need rum-api 0.2.0 or newer. Grafana provisioning (data sources, the app's settings with the read token) is not in the image.
