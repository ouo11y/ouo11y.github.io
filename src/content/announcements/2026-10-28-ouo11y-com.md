ouo11y now has a home at **ouo11y.com**. Here is what you will find:

- **[Principles](/docs/start/principles/)**: open source, OpenTelemetry compliant, end-to-end and unified, customizable all the way down, no pressure to use our SDK, and `ouo.*` names that give way to OpenTelemetry ones.
- **[Docs](/docs/)**: run the stack, instrument with our SDK or with plain OpenTelemetry, and change anything you do not like.
- **[Blog](/blog/)**: know-how, tips and tricks from building RUM on OpenTelemetry.
- **[Newsletter](/newsletter/)**: once a month, what changed in OpenTelemetry and across observability vendors, with sources.
- **Announcements**: this page. Releases and news from us.

Every section has an RSS feed. Questions: [hello@ouo11y.com](mailto:hello@ouo11y.com).
