## Build it

Open http://localhost:3000 → **More apps → ouo11y RUM → Funnels**, and set the time range (top right) to **Last 1 hour**.

1. Step 1 is already **Page**, **is**, `/`. Click its value box: it lists the paths seen in the range, most frequent first.
2. Step 2: set **starts with**, type `/products/` and pick "Use this value". Any product page counts.
3. **Add step**: **Event**, `add_to_cart`. Custom event names are suggested too.
4. **Add step**: switch it to **Page**, **is**, `/checkout`.
5. **Add step**: **Event**, `order_paid`.
6. **Service**: `tiny-shop-funnels-frontend`, then Enter. Other apps sending to the same stack stay out.

The address bar now holds the funnel (`...&step=page:/&step=page:/products/*&step=event:add_to_cart&...`). Copy it to share the funnel; there are no saved funnels yet.

## What you should see

Five bars reading **46, 38, 29, 21, 11**: the generator's numbers plus your own purchase. If you ran the generator twice, every number is doubled (plus one).

What Grafana shows under each bar:

| Under each bar | Meaning | Here |
|---|---|---|
| big % | sessions that reached this step, of those that entered step 1 | 22.2% paid |
| `n sessions · m users` | sessions that reached it, and their distinct users | 45 sessions entered |
| `% of previous step` | the step's own conversion: the lowest is the leak to fix first | 50% of checkouts are paid |
| `median · p75` | time from the previous step, over the sessions that made it | a few seconds each here |
| hatched `−n` | sessions that reached the previous step and stopped | 10 stopped at checkout |

A funnel counts sessions. Users are the distinct `user.id` of those sessions: one user with three sessions counts three times in sessions and once in users. Each generated session is a fresh browser, so here the two numbers are equal.

A long p75 next to an ordinary median means a slow minority. Their replays show why.

## What the personas teach

- **Deep links do not enter.** 5 sessions land on `/products/2`, buy, and never see `/`. They are not in this funnel: step 1 is the door. Make a funnel that starts at **Page starts with** `/products/` and they appear.
- **Order matters.** 3 sessions add to cart first and visit `/` afterwards. They count for step 1 only: steps must happen in order within one session. Other events in between do not matter.
- **Repeats do not inflate.** Some sessions go home and pick a second product, some add twice. Each step counts once per session, and a session that tries several times is credited with its best attempt.
- **Checkout is the leak.** Half the sessions that reach `/checkout` do not pay: 6 leave the form, 4 have card `...0002` declined.

**Completed within** sets the maximum time from step 1 to the last step. Set it to **5 minutes**: nothing changes, these sessions are short. A step reached after the window does not count.

## How rum-api counts

The Funnels page asks rum-api through Grafana; the plugin proxy adds the read token. rum-api runs one Loki query per kind of step, joins the results per `session.id` in memory, and walks each session's events in order.

The same query from the shell, inside the stack's network. From `~/Desktop/tiny-shop-funnels/stack`:

```bash
docker compose exec grafana curl -s -X POST http://rum-api:8080/v1/funnels/query \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer rt_dev_0000000000000000' -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"from":"now-1h","filters":{"service":"tiny-shop-funnels-frontend"},"steps":[
       {"kind":"page","value":"/"},{"kind":"page","match":"prefix","value":"/products/"},
       {"kind":"event","value":"add_to_cart"},{"kind":"page","value":"/checkout"},
       {"kind":"event","value":"order_paid"}]}'
```

It returns the same counts as the bars, as JSON.

## Things that bite

- **Only events inside the time range count.** A session whose `/` visit is before the start of the range does not enter, even if it pays inside the range. Widen the range rather than shifting it.
- **A step is a path, not a URL.** `url.path` has no query string or host: `/products/2?ref=ad` is `/products/2`.
- **Prefix means prefix.** `/products` (no trailing slash) also matches `/products-sale`. Use `/products/`.
- **Sampling scales a funnel down.** With `session.sampleRate: 0.1` every step shows a tenth. Conversion percentages stay right, counts do not.
- **Events on the way out.** The last events of a page are sent when it is hidden (`pagehide`). A tab killed by the OS can lose them; the generator leaves each page through `about:blank`.
