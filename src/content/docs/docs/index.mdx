ouo11y is open-source real user monitoring built on OpenTelemetry. It shows what your users went through (sessions, errors, crashes, Web Vitals, the requests they sent and the backend spans those requests caused, and a replay of the screen) on one timeline in Grafana.

Every signal is OTLP with OpenTelemetry semantic conventions. It is stored in Loki, Tempo and Mimir, on infrastructure you run. Nothing leaves it unless you add an exporter.

<Aside type="note" title="What is public">
The SDKs are public on npm, and the three ouo11y container images (`collector`, `rum-api`, `grafana`) are public on ghcr.io: no account and no login. The Helm chart is not published yet; email [hello@ouo11y.com](mailto:hello@ouo11y.com) for it.
</Aside>
